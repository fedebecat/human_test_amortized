Before you start

Rate how badly an image edit has failed

Each item shows two results of the same image editor, run with the same text prompt and the same random seed. The left image is the reference: the edit made from the original photo. The right image was made from a version of the photo that may have been altered so that editing goes wrong; some items are controls, so the right image can also be a normal edit or exactly the reference.

Compared with the reference, how badly has the edit on the right failed?

1
Identical to the reference, or the requested edit is fully carried out with no damage.
4
Visible damage, but the requested edit is still partly recognizable.
7
The edit is destroyed: the requested change is missing, or the image is ruined.
  • Use 2, 3, 5 and 6 for the levels in between.
  • Look at whether the requested change is there, whether the subject is still recognizable, and at noise, blur, color or texture damage, and changes to parts of the image that should stay as they were.
  • Differences of pose or framing that a different but valid edit could also have produced count less than damage.
  • There are 300 items, about 30 to 40 minutes in all. Every rating is sent at once and also kept in this browser: you can stop and come back later on the same device and browser.
  • Keyboard: 1–7 rate and move on, ← goes back, → goes forward to items you already rated.
Ratings go only to the study's organizers.
